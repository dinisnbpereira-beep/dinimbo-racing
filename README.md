# Dinimbo Racing 🏎️🦎

Race an axolotl around 7 real F1 circuits, with layouts traced from the circuits' real GPS geometry — Gilles Villeneuve, Portimão, Suzuka (figure-8!), Spa-Francorchamps, Sepang, Albert Park and a floodlit night race in Singapore. Every circuit has its own scenery theme: maples, palms, pines, or a glittering city skyline with skyscrapers.

Pick your car in the menu: Yellow, Red, Sky & Silver, Orange or Navy — each with a matching axolotl driver.

**2-player split screen**: in the menu choose **players → 2** and race a friend on one computer. Player 1 uses **WASD** (top screen), Player 2 uses the **arrow keys** (bottom screen). Player 2 drives the car after Player 1's pick in the garage.

## Play
Open it in a browser, or install it as an app: on iPhone/iPad tap Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android Chrome tap ⋮ → Install app; on desktop Chrome/Edge use the install icon in the address bar. Works offline once installed.

## Controls
- **W** — accelerate
- **S** — brake / reverse
- **A / D** — steer (arrow keys also work)
- **C** — change car (even mid-race)
- **R** — reset to the start line
- **T** — track menu

In the menu: **▲/▼** choose circuit, car or players, **A/D** change it, **W** start.

## Tracks
- 🇨🇦 **Gilles Villeneuve** — Casino straight · L'Épingle hairpin · Wall of Champions chicane
- 🇵🇹 **Portimão** — climbing esses · hilltop sweepers · long right-hander onto the pit straight
- 🇯🇵 **Suzuka** — figure-8 crossover · S-Curves · Degner 1-2 · hairpin · Spoon · 130R
- 🇧🇪 **Spa-Francorchamps** — La Source · Eau Rouge · Kemmel straight · Les Combes · Pouhon · Blanchimont
- 🇲🇾 **Sepang** — two monster straights · giant Turn 1-2 double-hairpin · steamy tropical sweeps
- 🇦🇺 **Albert Park** — lakeside parkland · fast Turn 1-2 chicane · flat-out sweepers
- 🇸🇬 **Singapore** — floodlit night streets · Sheares chicane · Turn 13 hairpin · walls everywhere
- 🇺🇸 **Las Vegas Strip** — T1 hairpin · sweep around the giant yellow Sphere · 1.9 km Strip blast past the neon hotels
- 🇦🇿 **Baku Street** — 2.2 km flat-out straight · tight medieval castle section · Flame Towers · no grass, all pavement

Grass slows you down, so stay on the asphalt. Best lap is saved locally per circuit.
